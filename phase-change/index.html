What happens as a substance is heated?

Select stages 1–5 on the graph or use the buttons. Assume a pure substance heated at constant pressure.

Focus: Temperature reflects average translational kinetic energy. During melting and boiling, supplied energy increases intermolecular potential energy instead of temperature.

Model simplification: Between phase changes, changes in intermolecular potential energy are treated as negligible. During the flat sections, temperature and average kinetic energy remain constant.
